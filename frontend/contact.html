Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your products organic?

All our products are certified organic, grown without synthetic pesticides, fertilizers, or GMOs. We follow strict organic farming standards and conduct regular testing to ensure quality and purity.

How long does shipping take?

We offer fast and reliable shipping across India. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days, while express shipping takes 1–2 business days. All orders include tracking.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with a product, contact our support team for a full refund or replacement — no questions asked.

Are your products vegan?

Most of our products are vegan. Each product page clearly indicates whether it's vegan or contains animal-derived ingredients. We also have a dedicated vegan section.

How can I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships. Use it to track your package in real-time on our Track Order page.

Can I place bulk or corporate orders?

Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk, corporate, and event orders. Contact us directly for a custom quote tailored to your needs.
